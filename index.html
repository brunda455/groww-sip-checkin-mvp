What this assistant can do

Explain common SIP operations in plain language.

  • Understand a failed instalment
  • Explain why early values can fluctuate
  • Compare the mechanics of reduce, pause, skip and stop
  • Point out when an official support channel is needed
It cannot tell you what to buy, predict returns, or decide whether you should stay invested.

About this MVP

Built from the supplied Groww growth PM case study. The proposed product is a calm SIP check-in that explains what numbers mean and makes reduce, pause or skip options easier to understand before an investor exits.

Included

  • Chat-style SIP check-in
  • English and Hinglish demo handling
  • Advice-request refusal guardrail
  • 20-case QA test lab
  • Metrics and guardrail dashboard
  • Serverless Gemini 2.5 Flash adapter

Not included

  • Groww login or account connection
  • Real payment/mandate repair
  • Fund recommendations or portfolio advice
  • Scraping public posts or personal data
  • Production analytics or user research
